## Step 4: weather per departure (founder: "MET Norway forecast, then climate averages for later dates")

### Decisions
- **One forecast for all departures:** one MET request for central Tbilisi (41.6934, 44.8015), because all tours are in the centre. It is stored in IndexedDB `meta` and fetched again only after `Expires` (about 30 min). After a failure the app waits 10 min, and it never fetches offline. Plain GET without custom headers, as MET requires for browsers.
- **Tour window:** the weather covers the tour itself, from start to start + product duration, in Tbilisi time (UTC+4, no DST). Hourly periods are used when they cover the whole tour; otherwise six-hour periods, labelled "(in 6-uursblokken)". The symbol shown is the most severe in the window.
- **Warnings:** heat ≥ 32 °C, rain ≥ 1 mm, thunder, wind ≥ 10 m/s, frost ≤ 0 °C, snow/sleet. In the climate view, months with an average maximum ≥ 30 °C get a heat hint.
- **Climate averages:** NOAA GHCN-Daily station GG000037545 Tbilisi, with no commercial restriction (citation only). The station's record is complete up to 1992; after that rain and minimum temperature are mostly missing, while maximum and mean temperature stay well covered (15–18 years per month). So temperatures use 2005–2024 (current climate; about 1–2° warmer than 1961–1990 in summer) and rain uses 1961–1990 (30 years per month, 493 mm a year). A month counts only with at most 5 missing days; the build fails below 10 years. Both periods are shown in the app.
- **Sunrise/sunset:** calculated locally with the NOAA solar formulas. It matches the MET sunrise API within 2 min on 2026-10-23 (07:21/18:07) and 2026-06-21 (05:26/20:38). There is a warning when a tour runs into the dark.
- Past dates and cancelled departures show no weather.

## Step 5: price profiles and finances (founder: "step 5")

### Decisions
- **No prices in code:** the private plan's proposed prices stay private. Every profile field defaults to 0, and the founder enters the amounts on the product page. They sync via the private data repo only. Tests use made-up amounts.
- **Profile on the product** (`TourProduct.pricing`, optional): older records get defaults through `pricingOf()`, and an older app keeps the field when it saves (spread). So no schema bump is needed.
- **Pricing:**
  - Per person: adults × adult price + children × child price + infants × infant price.
  - Private: a flat fee up to N paying guests, plus a price per extra guest. Infants are free.
  - Commission % per channel (not direct). With "ophogen" (on by default, following the plan) the price on a commission channel is direct ÷ (1 − rate), rounded to whole euros, so the net equals direct.
- **Bookings:** a new booking follows the profile until the founder types a price; typing a price recalculates the commission from the channel rate. An existing booking keeps its stored price and shows the profile's suggestion with "Overnemen".
- **Costs:**
  - The cost per guest counts paying guests of active bookings (not no-shows or cancellations). The cost per departure counts only when the departure runs (not cancelled, ≥ 1 paying guest).
  - Costs are derived from the current profile, so editing a profile changes past departures' costs. Revenue and commission are stored per booking and do not change. This is noted on the Financiën page.
- **Break-even:** ⌈fixed cost ÷ (adult price − cost per guest)⌉ adults; shown only for per-person products with a fixed cost.
- **Financiën page:** `#/geld/<year>`, linked from the Agenda header. Cancelled departures and bookings are left out. The CSV uses Dutch Excel style (semicolons, decimal comma, BOM) and has no names, contact details or notes.
- `departureMoney` was removed; it was replaced by `departureFinance`.
