# Session Breakdown — 2026-11-06-01

## Objective
Founder: list what is left and what is missing, and start a study section where clicked destinations give a mapped storyline and a story that flows naturally on tour.

## Completed
- New section **Studie** (`#/studie/<site,site,...>`, in the top menu):
  - Pick sites on the map or from a list of buttons. The selection lives in the address, so it survives a reload.
  - The storyline is drawn as numbered stops with straight dashed lines, labelled as not a walking route.
  - Four orders: Verhaallijn (distance, with a bonus for a shared thread), Korte route, Tijdlijn, Mijn volgorde.
  - The story: an opening, a passage per stop, a bridge between stops, and a closing, plus a "rode draad", speaking time and straight-line distance.
- 20 written passages for the central sites, with 15 threads linking them. The other 38 sites work too, but show their claims in a row, marked as not yet written.

## Decisions
- **No AI in the app.** A language model in the browser would need a paid API or a key in the client, which the zero-cost rule excludes. Instead Claude writes the text ahead of time from the claims, in `src/data/story-passages.json`, and the app chooses the order and the links. This works offline. The cost is that it can only recombine prepared text, so new wording needs a Claude Code session.
- **Sentence-level provenance.** Every sentence names its claims. A rejected claim removes the sentence and any bridge resting on it; a claim the guide edited marks the sentence wavy for checking. Sentences without claims are connecting text and add no facts. Passages were checked by hand against the claim texts; a test covers that all references exist, not that the wording is faithful.
- **Bridges come from real links in the sources:** gunpowder in Narikala and Metekhi, sculptor Amashukeli, poet Grishashvili, Jean Chardin, Agha Mohammad Khan, and so on. Each template is used at most as often as it has variants; without a link the bridge states the jump in time (`era`), or just names the next stop.
- **20 sites first,** so the founder can judge the writing before the other 38 are done.
- Concept underlines are off by default, because nearly every sentence is still concept and the underlining made the text hard to read. The count stays visible, with a "Toon wat nog concept is" switch.
