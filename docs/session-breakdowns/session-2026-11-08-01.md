## A-to-Z walkthrough as the founder (no code changed)
The founder asked for a full test: a party of 6 booked through Instagram for a history tour of the old town, from planning to walking it. This ran on the local build with test data and sync off. All test records were deleted afterwards with the app's own buttons.

2. Maak een tour. The route was calculated by itself: 2.7 km, 51 min.
3. Agenda → Touraanbod → new product.
4. Plan a departure, then add the booking (channel "Anders", Instagram noted in the notes).

7. Paid, Terugblik, Financiën, Cijfers.

Findings, most important first:
1. **Guide mode leaves the stop you stand at.** After "Aangekomen" or "Bevestigen", the screen jumps to the next stop and shows that stop's talking points. The points for where you are standing are only reachable with "Vorige". There is no "you are here, tell this" state.
2. **The transition and guide notes sit at the very bottom**, after all talking points. The guest questions from Studie end up as plain text in "notities".
3. **Three different durations:** Studie says about 14 min of telling, the tour 51 min, the product 180 (default) and the founder's own 150. The product does not take its duration or name from the chosen tour. Messages say "ongeveer 150 minuten".
4. **The meeting point is not part of the route.** Guide mode said 960 m from the meeting point to stop 1, and the tour starts at Abanotubani while guests wait at the clock tower.
5. **A booking takes three screens** (product, departure, booking). There is no quick "Nieuwe boeking" from Vandaag or the Agenda. The departure form defaults to the first product, not the newest or last used.
6. **No Instagram channel** and nowhere for an Instagram handle. A party of 6 cannot be marked private: the departure stays "6 van 10" open.
7. **Ending a tour does nothing elsewhere.** The departure stays "Gepland", Vandaag still shows "Start gidsen", and there is no end screen for paid, feedback and review. "← Tour" in guide mode goes to the editor, not to the departure or Vandaag. The review request only appears the next day.
8. **Saving is inconsistent:** the product page needs "Opslaan" at the bottom of a 2200 px page, with no warning when leaving unsaved; departure edits save themselves.
9. **"Nieuw touraanbod" creates a record at once**, so leaving it empty leaves a stray "Nieuw touraanbod" (confirmed).
10. Hard-to-find controls:
    - Touraanbod, Financiën and Cijfers are plain text links in the Agenda header, beside a second "Vandaag".
    - Terugblik and Vertrek wijzigen are folded at the bottom.
    - "Betaald" is 28 px high on a phone.
    - On a phone, Studie and Instellingen are off-screen in a nav that scrolls without a visible hint.
    - In the tour editor a stop card opens with the shared landmark fields before the talking points.
11. In dark guide mode, "Nog te betalen" is low contrast.

Worked well: Studie to tour kept all talking points, sources, transitions and questions. The walking route calculated by itself. Price prefill (6 × €25), the three messages with the weather and what to bring, check-in per person, GPS arrival prompts at all 9 stops, payment from guide mode, feedback, and the money totals all worked.

## Guide mode: "you are here" (founder chose "Gidsmodus eerst": findings 1, 2 and part of 7)
### Decisions
- **Two states per stop:**
  - *Onderweg naar*: name, walking time, distance, arrival note, Aangekomen and Overslaan; the talking points sit folded under "Vooruitkijken".
  - *Je bent hier*: the talking points are the page, with "Vragen en notities" folded under them. A last card holds the transition text and a large "Verder naar {next stop}" button, with the walking time to it.
  - Confirming arrival by GPS or by button moves to *Je bent hier*; it no longer jumps to the next stop.
- **Vorige always lands on a stop's story:** from the walk to stop 3, or from stop 3 itself, back to stop 2.
- **The end of the tour:** at the last stop the button reads "Tour afronden", which shows "Tour afgerond" with "Betaling en terugblik" (to the departure). The back link in the header goes to the departure when there is one, otherwise to the tour.
- The GPS arrival check only runs while walking. The route steps show while walking, and Achtergrond while standing at a stop.
- The progress saved per tour (localStorage) gained optional `here` and `ended` fields, so an older saved progress still loads, as "walking".
- Not done here: the departure status still stays "Gepland" after the tour (finding 7, part), and there is no time-versus-plan display.

## Design review of the whole app, then all points fixed (founder: "/design-review entire webapp", then "alle punten")
The review did not approve the app, for these reasons:
- two page-title styles (Public Sans 22 and Literata 26 or 28; two pages had none);
- sizes off the scale (12 and 13 px were the most common);
- 78 of 240 buttons in the editor in Arial;
- up to three brick buttons per screen;
- long, monotone lists;
- small hit areas;
- a 2274 px product page;
- forced-lowercase labels and Leaflet's own zoom buttons.

Two measured points turned out not to be defects and were left alone: the 2–3 px radii are pill ends on 4–6 px bars, and the 15 px guide-mode sizes are set by system.md.
