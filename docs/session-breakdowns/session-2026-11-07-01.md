## Step 6: guest check-in and messages (founder: "build it")
### Decisions
- **No schema bump:** `Booking.arrived`, `Booking.messagesSent` and the product fields are optional, and an older app keeps them when it saves.
- **Check-in counts per person** (a party of 3 can be 2 present). Undefined means not checked in yet. No-show sets 0; arriving after all undoes it.
- **Which departure:** the address (`#/gids/<tour>/<departure>`, from "Start gidsen"), otherwise today's first departure of a product using this tour. No departure means no guest list, so normal guiding is unchanged.
- **Messages never guess:** a missing meeting point shows `[verzamelpunt invullen]` and a warning; the forecast appears only when it reaches the tour (climate averages are no forecast); bad links are left out.
- **WhatsApp only for international numbers:** a local number such as 06... could reach the wrong person in another country. Those use copy.
- **To-do list on Vandaag:** confirmation for direct and WhatsApp bookings on a later day, reminder for tomorrow, review up to 7 days after the tour (not for no-shows). No contact means no reminder. "Sent" means copied or opened, because the app cannot know whether it really went out.
- Nothing from the private plan is in the code; messages use only the booking's own amount.

## Step 7: scouting and the rest of the plan (founder: "step 7 build it")
### Decisions
- **Schema 4:** blocked days are a new record type, so an old app must not overwrite them. Both devices must tap "Vernieuwen" after the deploy.
- **Warn, do not forbid:** a blocked day or an overlap disables "Vertrek plannen" until "Toch plannen" is ticked. An overlap is only one guide being in two places at once, so two tours that merely touch are fine. Cancelled and deleted departures do not count.
- **Practical info starts empty.** Nothing is bundled, because opening hours from the web go stale and may never be invented. A note needs text, a source and a date that is not in the future, and it is flagged after a year.
- **Product text names what is missing** instead of filling it in. Dutch only, as decided earlier.
- **Feedback has no names** and is kept for good, on the departure rather than on a booking.
- **Statistics count tours up to and including yesterday**, because a tour later today has not happened yet. Booking lead time uses the date entered, which the page states.
- Sites added and dropped: see `docs/RESEARCH.md`.
