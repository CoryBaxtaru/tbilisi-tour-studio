# Session Breakdown — 2026-11-15-01

## Objective
Before a first real test on the phone, the founder asked for four things:
- a standpoint per stop for places you cannot easily walk to (Kartlis Deda, Narikala);
- a clean slate;
- a tour on a guest's request, made through Claude with copy and paste;
- a recommended standard tour of about 2 hours.

## Completed
- **Standpoint per stop:** `TourStop.standpoint` holds a position and a note.
  - In the stop card, "Waar je gaat staan" lets the guide pick a spot on the map, describe it, or go back to the site itself.
  - The route, the map, the arrival check and the guide mode use the standpoint (`stopPosition` in `tourOps.ts`), and routes to and from that stop are recalculated (`withStandpoint`).
  - Guide mode shows "Standpunt …" while walking and at the stop.
- **"Alles wissen"** in Instellingen ("Opnieuw beginnen"), with counts and a confirmation step.
  - It deletes all tours, products, departures, bookings, expenses, blocked days and own places as tombstones, so the other device empties on its next sync.
  - Guests' details are blanked on the tombstones.
  - Research, verifications and stories on catalogue places stay.
  - Remembered guide progress and last choices are cleared.
- **Tour op maat met Claude** (Tours page):
  - the guest's wish and the length go into a prompt with the 66 catalogue sites (id, name, district, tags, position, note);
  - Claude replies in a fixed block (`=== TOUR ===` … `=== EINDE ===`), and the app checks it, reports unknown places and makes the tour;
  - talking points, transitions and guest questions come from the app's own story passages, so Claude only picks the order and says why;
  - why each stop is there and Claude's standpoint idea go into the guide notes;
  - no API and no cost: the founder uses Claude in a chat.
- **Recommended tour "Klassiek Tbilisi in 2 uur":** one tap on the Tours page.
  - Stops: Vrijheidsplein, Anchiskhati, Gabriadze clock tower, Bridge of Peace, cable car Rike–Narikala, Narikala, Kartlis Deda (told from the fortress), Abanotubani, Meidan, Sioni.
  - The reasons in it are about the route, not facts.
- Tours page: the recommended tour and the tour on request lead. The ready-made templates are folded, with no brick button.

## Decisions
- **The guide picks every standpoint position on the map.** Claude and the recommended tour only describe one in words, because coordinates are never invented.
- **The reset keeps research**, because it is shared knowledge, not the founder's test data. Own places go, since they only exist for tours.
- **No schema change:** `standpoint` is optional.
