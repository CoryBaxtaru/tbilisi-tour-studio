## Part 4 — Card deck tour builder

### Objective
Build tours by dragging site cards (about 50 Tbilisi sites, tagged by era and tour type) into a route and reordering them, as in Google Maps.

### Completed
- Site catalogue: 58 sites in `scripts/catalog-source.mjs`. Positions and Georgian names come from OpenStreetMap via Nominatim (`scripts/build-catalog.mjs`, 1 req/s, OSM object recorded). 7 bad or missing matches were fixed by hand (listed in `docs/CATALOG.md`), plus one district correction.
- Tag vocabulary (`src/domain/tags.ts`): 9 eras, 12 tour types, 8 districts. Filtering is OR within a group and AND across groups, plus accent-insensitive text search (including Georgian).
- Editor redesign:
  - route list with drag handles, walking time and distance between stops, and remove/↑/↓ buttons;
  - card deck panel (filters, + button, "stop N" badge for used cards);
  - map dots for unused filtered sites with an "Aan route toevoegen" popup, and focus-on-card;
  - automatic route recalculation 1.5 s after a change;
  - tag, district and verification editing in the stop card.
- Catalogue landmarks have fixed ids and timestamps, so there are no sync conflicts across devices.
- Fixed a latent bug: route results and edits now apply to the latest tour, not a stale render copy.
- Docs: `docs/CATALOG.md` (data status, licence, tags, how to add sites, review table), HOSTING and PROJECT_STATE updated.

### Verification

- Browser (production build, 1280×800):
